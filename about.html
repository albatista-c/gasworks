About

Designing so that complicated things are easy to follow.

I’m a designer and researcher. My projects range from a Kazakh language textbook, club logos, and linguistics posters at the University of Washington to a phonology database and nanotechnology research posters made during internships at Los Alamos National Laboratory.

I treat each one as a communication problem: who is reading, and what do they need to understand? Sometimes the answer is a striking image that makes someone pick up a book. Sometimes it is a protocol clear enough that anyone can follow it and get the same result.
